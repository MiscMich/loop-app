## Sounds Used

- tap.aif - Button press feedback
- select.aif - Selection toggle
- pop.aif - Light feedback (mood selection)
- success.aif - Content saved/exported
- send.aif - Content sent
- move.aif - Game move
- victory.aif - Game win
- error.aif - Error/game draw
- notification.aif - Incoming content
- record.aif - Recording start/stop
